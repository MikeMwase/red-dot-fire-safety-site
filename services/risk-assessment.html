Fire risk assessments

We assess your premises, identify fire safety risks and recommend the action needed to address them.

What's included

03 — on-site, and on file

  • On-site walkthrough of your premises
  • Identification of fire hazards and problem areas
  • Review of existing equipment, coverage and placement
  • Written report ranking risks by priority
  • Practical, costed recommendations — not a sales pitch
  • Follow-up support once corrective work is done

A good assessment tells you what needs attention now, what can wait, and what's already fine. That's the report you'll get.
