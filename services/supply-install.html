Fire equipment supply & installation

The right fire extinguishers and safety equipment, correctly selected and installed for your premises.

What's included

02 — on-site, and on file

  • Extinguisher type matched to the risk — water, foam, CO₂, dry powder or wet chemical
  • Fire blankets for kitchens and food-prep areas
  • Brackets, stands and signage supplied and fitted
  • Correct mounting height and placement for access and visibility
  • Replacement of outdated or non-compliant equipment
  • Walkthrough once installed, so your team knows what's where

Equipment is selected for what's actually on your premises — not a standard pack. You'll get a clear quotation before anything is installed.
